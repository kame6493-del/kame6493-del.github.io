Hunchy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Hunchy is a party game played on one device. We do not ask for an account, and we do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Player names, settings, which cards were already used and the date of each free daily game are saved only on your device (local storage). Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play / the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check which items your store account owns. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not your name or player names. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (or clear its storage) to remove everything saved on your device. To delete the anonymous purchase record kept by RevenueCat, email kame6493@gmail.com from the Google account you bought with, or include your order number, and we will delete it within 30 days. Purchases you made stay tied to your store account.

No ads, no tracking

Hunchy shows no ads and does not use advertising IDs or analytics SDKs.

Children

Hunchy does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
