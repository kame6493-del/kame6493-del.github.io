Doodle Sports Day Privacy Policy

October 17, 2026 (October 18: added the Full Version purchase; October 21: added contacts for deleting drawings)

This page is an English translation of the Japanese privacy policy.

This page explains the information handled by Doodle Sports Day (らくがき運動会; the Android and iPhone apps and the browser version, the "Game"). The Game is made by an individual developer.

Kept only on your device

  • The creatures you draw, race results, the album, personal bests and sound settings are kept on your device (the browser's or app's storage). They are not sent to the developer.
  • Drawings you tapped "Hide" on, and the anonymous number of the person who posted them, are also remembered only on your device (so that person's drawings are not shown).
  • Deleting the app deletes these as well.

Sent (only when you use these features)

Only when you use the following features, data is sent to Google Firebase (Firestore and anonymous sign-in).

  • Anonymous user ID: a number created automatically by Firebase anonymous sign-in. No name, email address, phone number, etc. is used. It is used for posting limits and to tell which posts are yours.
  • Drawings posted to "Everyone's Doodles": the line data, the creature's name and the date and time posted. They are shown on other people's screens.
  • Records listed in "Rankings": the line data, the creature's name and the time. They are shown on other people's screens.
  • Rooms in "3-player battle": the room code and the drawings and names made in that room. Only the people in the room see them.
  • Reports: which drawing you reported (so the same person cannot report many times).

Communication is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not use the collected information for advertising or sell it, and we do not give it to third parties.

Buying the Full Version (app versions only)

  • Purchases of the Full Version (a one-time in-app purchase) in the app versions are handled by Google Play or the App Store. Payment information such as card numbers does not reach the developer.
  • To check whether you have purchased, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). An anonymous user number created automatically by the app and the purchase record (product, date and time, store receipt) are sent to RevenueCat. No name or email address is sent.
  • "Restore Purchase" after reinstalling also uses this purchase record.
  • Pens and sparkle effects you choose in the Full Version are sent as part of the drawing data, only when you post to "Everyone's Doodles", "Rankings" or "Rooms" above.
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Not sent

  • We do not use location, contacts, the camera or microphone, or the photos on your device.
  • The app versions contain no ads. They also contain no usage analytics tools.
  • When saving a vertical video to your device, the app only writes to "Documents/RakugakiUndoukai" on Android and does not read any other files.

Sharing

When you share a result image or video, the Game only opens your device's share screen. You choose where to send it. Challenge links contain the line data of your drawing.

Ads in the browser version

The browser version (yurustudio.github.io/undoukai/) shows ads (Ninja AdMax, Google AdSense) on the title and result screens. The ad providers may use cookies and similar technologies.

If you want something deleted

If you want a drawing or name you posted to "Everyone's Doodles" or "Rankings" deleted, please send us the drawing's name and the date you posted it (and the event or time, if you know them) by email (kame6493@gmail.com) or on X (@apkderete). We will check it and delete it from the server. At the moment there is no button in the Game to delete your own drawings (we plan to add one in the next version).

You can also tell us about offensive drawings or names with "Report" in the Game. A drawing is removed from the lists after 3 reports.

Children

The Game is intended for ages 13 and up.

Changes

When we change this content, we will update this page.
